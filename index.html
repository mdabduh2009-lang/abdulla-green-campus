ABOUT GREEN CAMPUS

Green Campus is an initiative to create a clean and eco-friendly environment in our university. It encourages students to plant trees, save water, use renewable energy and reduce pollution.

OUR GREEN ACTIVITIES

Tree Plantation

Planting trees to make our campus green and fresh.

Solar Energy

Using sunlight to produce clean and renewable energy.

Water Conservation

Saving water and preventing unnecessary wastage.

Waste Management

Recycling waste and keeping the environment clean.

Organic Gardening

Growing plants and protecting the natural environment.

Nature Protection

Protecting trees, plants and natural resources.

BENEFITS OF GREEN CAMPUS

  • Provides fresh and clean air.
  • Reduces environmental pollution.
  • Saves water and electricity.
  • Protects trees and plants.
  • Creates a healthy learning environment.
  • Promotes awareness among students.

OUR VISION

Our vision is to create a clean, green and sustainable campus where every student contributes towards protecting nature.
